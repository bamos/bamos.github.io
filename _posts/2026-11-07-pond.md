The pond on my [homepage](/) is a small place to play: leaves float, fish swim, and clicking and dragging leaves a soft wake. This is a closer look at its pieces.

The ripple examples below are still frames. Pick up the creatures and spheres in the interactive examples, then drop them back into the water.

## Clicks

A click sends out one expanding ripple. It stays circular, with small natural variations each time.

## Trails

Click and drag across open water to leave a silk ribbon that gently spreads and fades over about ten seconds. Moving the pointer alone leaves the pond still.

## Fields

Currents winds around a metallic sphere. Lorenz traces two lobes, with red and blue particles each making a complete circuit. Drag the spheres to bend the paths around them. The Möbius band has one half twist and a single centerline. The fields stay in place while their particles move.
