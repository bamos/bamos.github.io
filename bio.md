Brandon Amos is a Member of Technical Staff in post-training at [Reflection AI](https://www.reflection.ai/) in NYC. His research spans machine learning, optimization, reinforcement learning, and control with the goal of building safe intelligent systems that understand and interact with our world.

Previously, he was a Research Scientist in Fundamental AI Research
(FAIR) at [Meta Superintelligence Labs](https://ai.meta.com/research/),
a Visiting Lecturer at [Cornell Tech](https://tech.cornell.edu/), and has also worked at
[Adobe Research](https://research.adobe.com), [Google DeepMind](https://www.deepmind.com/), and [Intel Labs](https://www.intel.com/content/www/us/en/research/overview.html).
He holds a Ph.D. in Computer Science from [Carnegie Mellon University](https://www.cmu.edu/),
where he was supported by the National Science Foundation Graduate Research Fellowship Program,
and a B.S. in Computer Science from [Virginia Tech](https://www.vt.edu/),
where he was supported by nine scholarships.
He has received an Honorable Mention for the 2026
INFORMS Computing Society Prize, best paper awards at the
ICML Theoretical Foundations Workshop and ACM MMSys,
best reviewer awards at NeurIPS, ICML, ICLR, and AISTATS,
and is an area chair for AAAI, AISTATS, ICML, ICLR, and NeurIPS.
